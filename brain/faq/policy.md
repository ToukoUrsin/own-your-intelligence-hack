# FAQ: policy

Answers for `policy` questions (procedure: search the FAQ, pick the matching answer, explain it in your own words).

- **Cancelling subscriptions:** Cancel any time; stays active until the end of the paid period.
- **Late subscription payment:** Extensions depend on membership (`policies/membership`).
- **Refund policy:** Refunds to card, account credit, gift card or check; 5–7 business days (`policies/returns-refunds`).
- **Return policy:** Depends on membership level (`policies/membership`).
