# FAQ: jeans

Answers for `jeans` questions (procedure: search the FAQ, pick the matching answer, explain it in your own words).

- **Grass stain:** Pre-treat with enzyme stain remover, wash cold.
- **Washing frequency:** Every 5–10 wears.
- **Leg length:** 32-inch inseam standard; 30 and 34 available.
- **Tailoring cost:** $15 hemming at our partner tailors.
- **Shrink:** Up to 1% after the first wash.
- **Colours:** Dark blue and black.
- **Larger/smaller sizes:** Waist 26–40 online.
- **Ripped design:** Yes, a distressed version exists in each line.
