# FAQ: membership

Answers for `membership` questions (procedure: search the FAQ, pick the matching answer, explain it in your own words).

- **Levels:** Guest, Bronze, Silver, Gold.
- **Premium qualification:** Spend $500 in a calendar year for Gold, $250 for Silver, $100 for Bronze.
- **Benefits:** Easier returns, fee waivers, promo code replacement, free overnight shipping with Premium (`policies/membership`).
- **Duration:** One year from the date the level was reached; renews automatically when the spend threshold is met again.
