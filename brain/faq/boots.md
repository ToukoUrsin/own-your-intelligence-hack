# FAQ: boots

Answers for `boots` questions (procedure: search the FAQ, pick the matching answer, explain it in your own words).

- **Paint stain:** Let it dry, gently scrape, then use a little rubbing alcohol on a cloth; test on a hidden spot first.
- **Width:** Standard (D) width; half sizes run true to size.
- **Gum:** Freeze with an ice cube, then peel it off.
- **Wearing in:** About 2 weeks of daily wear.
- **Waterproof:** Water resistant, not fully waterproof; reapply protector spray monthly.
- **Lace colour:** Brown laces, with a spare black pair in the box.
- **Size not in stock:** We restock every 2 weeks; the customer can sign up for a restock email on the product page.
- **Warranty:** 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
