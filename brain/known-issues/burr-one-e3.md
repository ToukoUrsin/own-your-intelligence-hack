# Burr One E3
Cause: usually oily dark-roast beans or a small stone jamming the burrs.
Fix steps: 1) unplug, 2) remove hopper, 3) turn the setting ring to 40 (coarsest), 4) remove top burr with the included key and brush out, 5) reassemble, run empty for 5 seconds.
If E3 returns after cleaning: warranty replacement of the motor module (ship part SKU BO-MTR-2, customer installs with 4 screws, or send refurbished unit).
