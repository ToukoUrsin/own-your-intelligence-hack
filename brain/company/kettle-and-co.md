# Kettle & Co

Online shop for home and café coffee equipment and beans: grinders (Burr One, Burr Mini), Pour Kettle Pro, Dripper Two, Brew Scale, filters, roasted beans and a bean subscription. Warehouse and pickup showroom: 1200 Embarcadero W, Oakland, CA.

- Support hours: 7 days a week, 8:00–20:00 Pacific. Human agents answer chat and phone in those hours; email within one business day.

## Account plans
- **Standard** (free): order history, saved addresses and cards, newsletter optional. Customers may call it "free", "basic" or "freemium".
- **Plus** ($5/month or $50/year): free shipping on every order, 10% off beans, early access. Customers may call it "premium", "gold", "pro" or "platinum".
- **Business** (free, for cafés and offices, approved by sales): Net-30 invoicing, volume pricing, free shipping. Customers may say "company", "corporate" or "business" account.
