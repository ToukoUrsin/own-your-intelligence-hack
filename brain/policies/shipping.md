# Shipping and delivery policy — Kettle & Co

- Orders placed before 14:00 PT on weekdays ship the same day from our Oakland warehouse. Orders show status **processing** until the label is created, then **shipped**.
- Delivery options (US): UPS Ground 3–5 business days (free over $60 or for Plus and Business; otherwise $6), UPS 2nd Day ($14), UPS Next Day ($29, order before 12:00 PT), free pickup at the Oakland showroom (ready in 2 hours during business days).
- Canada, UK and EU: DHL Express 5–8 business days, $29 flat (free for Plus over $150). Import duties and VAT for UK/EU/Canada are paid by the customer on delivery; DHL holds the parcel until they are paid.
- A tracking email is sent when the label is created. Tracking can show "label created" for up to 24 hours before the first scan.
- **Lost package rule:** if tracking has not updated for 5 business days, or the carrier marks it delivered but the customer did not receive it, we open a carrier trace and immediately reship at no cost (orders under $300) or refund. Orders over $300 need a carrier trace result first (up to 3 business days).
- **Address changes:** while an order is processing we change the shipping address directly. After the label is created we cannot change it; the customer can redirect via UPS My Choice or DHL On Demand Delivery.
