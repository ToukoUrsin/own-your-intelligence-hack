# Shipping policy

- Shipping statuses: **order received** (not shipped; address, date, item and payment can still be changed), **in transit**, **out for delivery**, **delivered**.
- Standard delivery takes 3–7 days. A package can be marked delivered a couple of days before it actually arrives: if the customer has waited 7 days or less, ask them to wait; after more than 7 days, reship the missing item (`procedures/shipping-issue-missing`).
- Overnight shipping costs $15. Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping; otherwise standard shipping is $5.
- Address changes are only possible before shipping (`procedures/shipping-issue-manage`, `procedures/manage-account-manage-change-address`).
