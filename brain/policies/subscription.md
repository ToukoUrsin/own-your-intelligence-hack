# Premium subscription

- The Premium subscription costs **$99 per year** and includes free overnight shipping, early access to collections and member pricing.
- `subscription_status` returns active/cancelled, the amount due and the due date. Customers can pay any part of the amount due (`procedures/subscription-inquiry-manage-pay-bill`).
- Late payment: extensions depend on membership (`policies/membership`). Cancel any time; the subscription stays active until the end of the paid period.
