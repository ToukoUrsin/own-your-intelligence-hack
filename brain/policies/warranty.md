# Warranty

- Grinders (Burr One, Burr Mini): 2 years parts and labor. Kettles (Pour Kettle Pro): 1 year.
- Warranty claims need the order number and a short video or photo of the problem.
- First try the troubleshooting steps in known issues. If they fail, we send a replacement part or a refurbished unit; the customer returns the faulty unit with a prepaid label.
- Not covered: damage from grinding non-coffee items, water damage to grinders, drops.
