# Website troubleshooting

Standard sequence for site problems (card declined, cart not updating, search not working, slow site): 1) ask the customer to try again (`try_again`), 2) log out and back in (`log_out_in`), 3) for card or cart issues enter the details and place the order for them (`enter_details`, `make_purchase`); for search or speed issues give instructions (clear cookies/cache, other browser) and, if it still fails, notify the website team (`notify_team`).
