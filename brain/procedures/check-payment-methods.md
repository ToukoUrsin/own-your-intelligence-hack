# Accepted payment methods

Intent `check_payment_methods` (PAYMENT). Customers say: what payment methods do you accept, can I pay with PayPal, pay in installments.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Klarna installments for orders over $100 (US only). Business accounts can pay on Net-30 invoice. No cash, checks, crypto or bank transfer for consumer orders. Saved methods are listed by `find_customer`.
