# Cancellation fees

Intent `check_cancellation_fee` (CANCEL). Customers say: early termination fee, cancellation penalty, fee to cancel.

1. Orders: no cancellation fee before shipping. After shipping it's a return ($8 label).
2. Plus plan: monthly Plus can be cancelled any time, no fee. Yearly Plus: prorated refund of unused full months, no penalty.
3. Bean subscription: no fee; cancel before the next billing date.
4. If they want to cancel something now, follow [[procedures/cancel-order]] or [[procedures/switch-account]].
