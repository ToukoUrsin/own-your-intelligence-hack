# Reviews and feedback

Intent `review` (FEEDBACK). Customers say: leave a review, give feedback, rate a product, comment on service.

1. Product reviews: on the product page when logged in (verified purchase badge). We send a review link 7 days after delivery.
2. Feedback about service: `create_case` type feedback with their text; thank them.
3. Negative feedback with a concrete problem → handle as [[procedures/complaint]].
