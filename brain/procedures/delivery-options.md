# Delivery options

Intent `delivery_options` (DELIVERY). Customers say: what shipping methods do you have, express delivery, pickup, ship to my country.

1. Answer from [[policies/shipping]]: UPS Ground, 2nd Day, Next Day, Oakland pickup; DHL Express for Canada, UK, EU.
2. Mention free shipping thresholds and that Plus members ship free.
3. We do not ship outside US, Canada, UK and EU.
