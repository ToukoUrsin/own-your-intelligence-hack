# Newsletter subscribe / unsubscribe

Intent `newsletter_subscription` (SUBSCRIPTION). Customers say: subscribe to newsletter, stop emails, unsubscribe.

1. `set_newsletter` for the sender's email (subscribed true or false). Works without an account.
2. The newsletter is every two weeks: new roasts, brewing guides, early sales. Order emails continue regardless.
