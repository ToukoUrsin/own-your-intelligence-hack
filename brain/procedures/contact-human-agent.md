# Talk to a human

Intent `contact_human_agent` (CONTACT). Customers say: speak to a person, real agent, human please, representative.

1. Offer to solve it now if the question is clear.
2. If they still want a person: `create_case` type callback with a one-line summary; a human replies within 2 hours in support hours (7 days 8:00–20:00 PT). Also give the phone number from [[company/kettle-and-co]].
