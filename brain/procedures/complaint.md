# Complaints

Intent `complaint` (FEEDBACK). Customers say: I want to complain, file a complaint, unhappy with service, bad experience.

1. Apologize and find the related order if any.
2. Fix what policy allows now (late or lost parcel → [[policies/shipping]]; damaged or wrong item → [[policies/returns-refunds]]).
3. `create_case` type complaint with a summary so the team lead follows up within 2 business days. Give the case id.
