# Refund policy questions

Intent `check_refund_policy` (REFUND). Customers say: what is your refund policy, can I get money back, return window.

Answer from [[policies/returns-refunds]]: 30-day returns of unused items, $8 return label, damaged/wrong items refunded free, refund timing 3–5 business days after the warehouse receives it, warranty for defects. No action needed unless they name an order.
