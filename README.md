# Own Your Intelligence Hackathon

Y Combinator, San Francisco · Sunday, September 27, 2026 · [event page](https://events.ycombinator.com/gbrain-qm-river-memorable-hackathon)

## Official description

Hosted by River AI, GBrain, Memorable, QM, Superset, and UFO, this hackathon is a day for building at the frontier of AI-native software.

Participants will explore new ways to extend QM and GBrain, build novel agent workflows and interfaces, experiment with multiplayer and software-factory ideas, and use River AI, Memorable, Superset and UFO to push beyond what today’s tools make easy. The goal is to make something ambitious, useful, or unexpected.
